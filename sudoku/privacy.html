Sudoku Kids — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Sudoku Kids (“we”, “the app”) is a fully offline children's sudoku game. Protecting children and families is the core design principle of this app. This policy explains in plain language what data the app does and does not handle.

1. We collect no personal information

The app does not collect, transmit, store on a server, or share any personal information. We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, photos, or any other identifying information.

2. The app has no internet access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission and contains no third-party networking code. Nothing you do in the app can leave the device over a network. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

3. No advertising and no tracking

The app contains no ads, no advertising identifiers, no tracking pixels, and no behavioral profiling.

4. Data stored on your device only

Game progress, settings (language, sound, vibration), coins, unlocked themes, and statistics are stored locally on the device using Android's local storage (DataStore Preferences). This data never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app removes this data; the Android system backup may include app data if you have backup enabled, under Google's own backup policies.

5. Permissions

  • VIBRATE — used only to provide gentle haptic feedback during play. It can be turned off at any time in the in-app settings.

No other permissions are requested.

6. Children

The app is designed for children (including ages under 13 and the Google Play Families audience). It is fully playable without supplying any personal information, works entirely offline, and contains no in-app purchases or ads in this version. A parent gate protects the parent-oriented sections.

7. Data safety summary

  • Data collected: none.
  • Data shared: none.
  • Network transmission: none (no internet access).
  • Data deletion: uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version with a new effective date will be posted at this address.
