Peaceful — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026
Developer: Tidbit Labs

Peaceful (“the app”) plays sleep sounds, mixes, a sleep timer, and breathing exercises. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

1. No account and no collection

The app does not ask you to create an account. It does not collect, transmit to us, or share personal information. There are no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

2. No network access

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. Sounds are generated on the device or played from audio files shipped inside the app. Nothing you do in the app is sent to a server.

3. Data stored on your device only

The following stay on the device, using Android DataStore:

  • your current mix and saved presets
  • the fade-out setting and language choice
  • listening statistics (minutes, session count, and a local streak)

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. If Android backup is enabled for this app, Google’s backup may include it under Google’s own policies.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications — optional, so the playback controls can appear while the app is in the background.
  • Foreground service (media playback) — keeps the mix playing after you leave the app.
  • Wake lock — keeps audio generation running while a mix is playing.

5. Purchases

This version has no ads and no in-app purchases. If a paid upgrade is added later, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

6. Data safety summary

  • Data collected: none.
  • Data shared: none.
  • Network transmission: none.
  • Data deletion: uninstall the app.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text and a new effective date will be posted at this address.
